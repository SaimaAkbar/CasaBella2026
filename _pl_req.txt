==================================================
1. DEFINE ACCOUNTING MODES
==================================================

The system may support:

CASH VIEW

and

ACCRUAL VIEW

Keep both if they already exist.

Definitions:

CASH VIEW:

Income =
actual qualifying business money received during selected period

Expenses =
actual qualifying business money paid during selected period

Result:

Net Cash Flow =
Cash Income - Cash Expenses

ACCRUAL VIEW:

Income =
earned/recognized business income for selected period

Expenses =
recognized business expenses for selected period

Result:

Net Profit / Loss =
Accrued Income - Accrued Expenses

Do NOT mix cash income with accrued expenses.

Do NOT label cash flow as profit unless the accounting basis actually supports that.

- unpaid balance as cash income
