==================================================

A Monthly Tenant is a person or business that takes one or more rooms/apartments from the organization on a monthly rental agreement.

The tenant must pay rent to the organization every month.

Example:

Tenant: Ahmed Khan

Assigned units:
- Casa Bella — Apartment 1A — Floor 1
- Casa Bella — Room 102 — Floor 1

Monthly rent:
- Apartment 1A = Rs. 40,000
- Room 102 = Rs. 20,000

Total monthly rent payable by tenant:
Rs. 60,000

The tenant may pay:

- advance
- partial payment
- half payment
- full payment
- multiple installments

The system must show:

- monthly amount payable
- amount received
- remaining/due amount
- due date
- overdue amount
- payment status
- payment history

15. ROLE RULES
==================================================

SUPER_ADMIN:

- assign or remove units

- create tenant/agreement/assignment

- cannot approve their own hotel-use settlement

RECEPTIONIST:

- view operational tenant and assigned-unit information
- cannot view full tenant finance or confidential CNIC unless specifically allowed
- cannot change rent
- cannot approve settlement
- may create/check-in a hotel guest only after approval
